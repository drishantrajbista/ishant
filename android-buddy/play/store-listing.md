**Full description** (2663/4000):

Buddy is a voice assistant that floats over your phone as a small bubble. Hold the bubble, ask a question about whatever is on your screen, and let go. Claude answers out loud, and Buddy can draw circles, boxes and arrows on your screen to show you exactly where to look. With Claude API mode it can also do things for you: open apps, tap, type, scroll and change settings when you ask.

HOW IT WORKS
- Hold the bubble and speak. Let go to send.
- Buddy looks at your screen at that moment (only then) and passes it, with your question, to Claude.
- You hear the answer, and a cursor flies across the screen, pointing and drawing.

TWO WAYS TO USE CLAUDE
- Claude app (free): Buddy opens the Claude app on your phone with your screenshot and question. Send it, copy Claude's answer, tap the bubble, and Buddy reads it aloud and draws on your screen. Uses your normal Claude account. No API key.
- Claude API (pay per use): bring your own Anthropic API key. Buddy talks to Claude in the background, answers faster, and can control your phone. Haiku 5.5 is the fastest and cheapest; Sonnet 5.5 and Opus 5.5 are smarter for long tasks.

VOICE
- Phone speech recognition, or Gemini (optional, free key from Google AI Studio) for the most accurate recognition of accents and mixed languages. Your recording is sent to Google only if you choose Gemini.
- Spoken replies in your language, with an adjustable speed.

LET CLAUDE CONTROL YOUR PHONE (optional)
In Claude API mode you can switch on Buddy's accessibility service. Then you can say things like "open YouTube and search lofi" or "turn on dark mode", and Claude taps, types and scrolls to do it. It only acts when you ask, you can stop it at any moment by tapping the bubble, it asks you out loud before sending, paying, posting or deleting, and it never types into password fields.

YOUR PRIVACY
- Screenshots are taken only when you release the bubble.
- No ads, no analytics, no account with us. Nothing is sent to the developer.
- Your API keys are stored encrypted on your device and are only sent to the provider they belong to.

DISCLOSURE: THIS APP USES THE ACCESSIBILITY SERVICE
Buddy uses Android's Accessibility Service only if you turn it on, and only to read what is on your screen and to tap, swipe, type and navigate on your behalf when you ask by voice. Screen content is sent to Anthropic (Claude API mode) to work out what to do. It is not stored, logged or shared with anyone else. You can turn the service off at any time in Settings > Accessibility.

Buddy is an independent app and is not made by or affiliated with Anthropic or Google. It is based on the open-source Clicky (MIT) by Farza.
