# Brag plan: Buddy for Android

**What it is:** Buddy is an Android take on Clicky, the macOS AI buddy: a floating bubble you hold to ask a question about whatever is on screen. Claude answers, Buddy speaks it, and a cursor flies over and points at the thing.
**For whom:** Anyone who's ever been stuck on their own phone ("where is that setting?").
**What sets it apart:** It doesn't just answer, it points at the right spot on your screen, and in Claude API mode it taps it for you. It understands Nepali and English and always answers in English.
**Most impressive claim:** Hold, ask, and watch a cursor find and flip the setting for you.
**Visual hook:** The coral cursor zooming out of the bubble and circling "Display".
**Real UI shown:** The bubble (frosted circle, waveform ring, thinking arc), the coral cursor with white halo, the drawn circle, the reply card, the "Doing: …" status, the real app icon, real example commands from the README ("YouTube khola", "set an alarm for 7am", "what's my day?").
**Tone:** default: punchy, playful, clean, in the app's own palette (#F2F2F7 grouped background, coral #D97757).
**Share caption:** Hold the bubble, ask out loud, and a cursor flies across your phone and points at the answer.

**Angle:** "Clicky, but it lives on your phone." Show it in use on a Settings task, end on the icon.

**Sound:** a bright 120 BPM groove in C major (soft pad, pluck arpeggio, light kick and hats). Effects are tuned to the same key and sit under the music: a soft thump on the press, an airy whoosh on cursor flights, a blip on arrival, small clicks on taps, pops on chips, a chime on the outro.
